# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS. Pinned by the brief for an empty project.

## Users

- **XIE students** (primary): undergraduates at Xavier Institute of Engineering, Mahim, Mumbai, usually on a phone between lectures. They want to know what XIE-CSI does and when the next event is.
- **Faculty and sponsors**: open the page to judge whether the chapter is organised and credible.
- **Members**: will later need a Member Login. Authentication and the dashboard are not built yet.

## Product Purpose

The official public homepage of XIE-CSI, the Computer Society of India student chapter at Xavier Institute of Engineering. Success: a student understands what the chapter does and finds the next event within the first two screens.

## Positioning

XIE-CSI is a student-led chapter that runs real events on campus (hackathons such as RepoForge, hands-on workshops, tech talks). It is the XIE branch of CSI, the national body, and not a generic coding club.

## Operating Context

- Events happen on campus in named rooms (for example, the IPL Lab).
- The committee changes each academic year. The current list is the 2026–27 student chapter tracker.
- Event details change often and must be editable without touching layout code.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- First build covers the public homepage only. Member Login shows a "Coming soon" state until a login route exists.
- Event registration links appear only when a real URL is supplied.
- Undecided: registration platform, member login provider, official contact email, and social handles.

## Brand Commitments

- Official XIE and CSI logos, shown together, at their original proportions and colours. **Not yet supplied to the project**: expected at `public/brand/`.
- Accent colour to be drawn from the supplied logos.
- Voice: plain, confident, student-led. No hype.

## Evidence on Hand

- Committee 2026–27 names and roles: from `XIE_CSI_Team_Work_Tracker.docx` (user's Downloads).
- Upcoming event: DSA Workshop, 23–27 November 2026, 10 AM–12 PM, IPL Lab, led by Saad Ansari, capacity 45.
- RepoForge 2026 took place. No verified attendance, sponsors, testimonials, results, or photos yet. **Do not fabricate them.**
- All photos are temporary Wikimedia Commons placeholders, not XIE coverage.

## Product Principles

1. The next event is never more than a scroll away.
2. Only verified facts. A missing fact stays missing instead of being invented.
3. Credible to faculty without feeling corporate to students.
4. Content lives in data files a future committee can edit.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Full keyboard path, visible focus, and reduced motion that shows all content immediately. Good on low-end Android phones over mobile data.
